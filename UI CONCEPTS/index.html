Shared tokens

All four concepts draw from the palette already written into docs/design.md.

Black#0A0A0B
Surface#121214
Surface high#232328
Gold#D4AF37
White#F7F7F5
Grey#A1A1A8
Done#5FD4A0
Scheduled#7FA8FF
Failed#FF7A70

How state reads. Gold is the accent and also means running now. Green is finished, blue is scheduled, coral is failed, grey is idle. Green, blue and coral never carry brand meaning, so they stay free to signal status anywhere in the product.
